Ferrari California

4.3L V8 · 460 hp reference · 310 km/h reference top speed

Ferrari V8. The original California uses a 4.3-litre naturally aspirated V8 producing 460 hp.
Fast and elegant. Ferrari quoted a 310 km/h top speed for the original model.

03 · The circuit

Three Sisters Circuit.

The Jewel of the North

This isn't a painted loop in a car park. Three Sisters is a proper motorsport venue with decades of racing history behind it.

Built on reclaimed colliery land in Wigan, Three Sisters became one of the North's best-known outdoor circuits. It is licensed by Motorsport UK and the ACU, with a longest configuration of 1,473 metres.

Future stars came through here. The circuit's history includes links to Lewis Hamilton, while Lando Norris and George Russell were regulars during their karting years.
Real racing pedigree. Motorcycle world champion Neil Hodgson took his first race win at Three Sisters in 1990.
Old-school character. The site grew out of land once dominated by three huge colliery spoil heaps — the "Three Sisters" that gave the circuit its name.
The best bit. Ryan gets to drive two genuine supercars on a circuit where serious racers learned their craft long before the big stages.
