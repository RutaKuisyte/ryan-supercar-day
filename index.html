03 · The circuit

Three Sisters Circuit.

A proper licensed circuit with real motorsport history.

Three Sisters is a Motorsport UK and ACU-licensed outdoor circuit in Wigan. The longest configuration is 1,473 metres.

The venue has links to Lewis Hamilton, Lando Norris and George Russell from their earlier racing years.
